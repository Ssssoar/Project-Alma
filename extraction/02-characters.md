# Character List
This will be a list of characters and their concepts, as well as possible relationships, story significance and arcs.

Because of the [two-world structure](01-concept.md) of the world, most characters will have two versions; one from the old save (the corrupted version) and one from the new save.

Both versions of the characters are understood to stem from the same branching points, with the events of each world/save file being what led them on the path to become each version of themselves. For the old save, the abandonment of the player is the biggest catalyst.

Most characters are listed by their role/archetype in the story rather than having a name.

## The Mentor
- Forward moving, a man of action.
- Extremely goal oriented.
- Always keeping his objectives in mind, always striving to fulfill them.
### In the new save
- He is a stoic guide.
- Pushes the player towards the original quest, to defeat the dragon that tries to conquer the land.
- Once the dragon dies and the corruptions appear he might develop a rigid attitude, trying to make sure you kill them as much as possible.
- The encounter with his old-save version might change this attitude to something more reasonable.
### In the old save
- He is a wild eyed, desperate seeker.
- Wants at all costs to find the player of the old save.
- He might become quite wicked in the search of this objective, since it's an end that justifies all means in his head.
- Senses something of the player of the old save in the one of the new one.

## The Dragon King Vilú
- A sly conqueror.
- Defined by his ambition.
- A representation of resentment and bitterness, and how they can act as a driving force for good if controlled.
- Loved by his subordinates. Despite being seen as evil, is actually a very good ruler and protector of his lands.
- Just that, you know, they're HIS lands.
- Has a very close relationship to his immediate subordinates, the four Serpent Generals
### In the new save
- He is the inciting event for the player's adventure.
- He is trying to conquer all the land.
- The quest to defeat him takes much of the early game.
- He is unceremoniously killed by his counterpart when the intrussions begin to happen.
- His loss leaves most of his lands in disarray.
- Might only be a fakeout, he would appear as an ally towards the end of the game.
### In the old save
- Known as Chimera-Vilú
- He is a hulking, monstrous, unthinking abomination.
- He has fused with his minions and two of his serpent generals.
- Like a monster, it has no objective.
- Has a dragon head and 2 serpent heads, those of his absorbed generals.
- The three heads constantly fight and hurt each other, and as a result the Chimera is in constant bleeding pain.
- Killing it's counterpart is the first thing it does as soon as it enters the new world.
- Might be the final boss of Act 2.
- There might be a misunderstanding about whether he is leading the corruptions from the other world, that would be resolved when upon his death, the chaos doesn't stop.

## The Wizard Sage
- A wise man and a recluse.
- Lives somewhere hard to reach and away from people.
- Maybe in a tall wizard tower in a cliff by a raging sea.
- Known as an incredibly powerful mage, though his existance might not be certain to people.
- His trust in the player is his most important aspect for the story.
### In the new save
- As part of the original quest to defeat the dragon, he was supposed to entrust the player some wisdom or secret that would aid him. Maybe the Dragon's weakness.
- With the Dragon defated, his secret doesn't have much use.
- It might be that the player seeks him hoping he can help with the intrussions, only to be disappointed when he can't or won't.
- His encounter with his alternate version might give him some insight as to what is going on with the intrusions from the other world.
### In the old save
- Affected by the abandonment of the player of the old save
- Had at first decided to have faith that the old player would return, but after enough time had passed and the dragon became a hulking abomination, decided to take things in his own hands.
- He develops and uses the most powerful magic in the world to open a portal to "a different past"
- He is the reason the abominations are flowing into the new save: The portals he opened are what's making everything flow towards it.
- He did this out of a belief that the player could be found in the new save, and fix things.
- Crumbles when he realizes the player of the new save is not the one that had abandoned his.
- Plagued by his guilt, since he believes he has doomed both worlds with his actions.

## The Scribe Archivist
- A man concerned with documents and writings.
- Might own or keep a library, has a strong belief in the power and importance of keeping knowledge.
- A very knowledgeable person, though not really an expert in many topics.
### In the new save
- A calm presence.
- Might have a sidequest involving bringing him books and archives from around the world.
- Might be a source of information for various legends, particularly regarding the saints, and rumors about the wizard.
- Forced to flee his library once the intrusions from the other world begin appearing.
### In the old save
- A maniacal hoarder of any and every scrap of writing.
- Has a mistaken belief that somwhere, somehow, there's a piece of writing that he can discover that can restore his old broken world.
- Burdened by his papers and books, yet refuses to let go of even a little bit.
- Forms a toxic relationship to the corrupted form of [The Scholar](#the-scholar).
- Grows increasingly frustrated as none of the writing that he hoards and reads brings him any closer to solving the problem in his world.
- Eventually lashes out, burning all the writing that he so jealously kept.
- After the fires are set to the library, he panics and regrets it.
- He might try to save everything from the fire.
- He forever might mourn everything he lost in the fire.
- He might have gone through this cycle of hoarding -> burning -> regret many times in the past.

## The Casanova
- A Boisterous, over-the-top ladies' man.
- Big muscles, chiseled jaw, Johnny Bravo type swagger.
- Loves to flex, loves to work out, loves to show off.
- Playfully antagonistic and competitive.
- Surrounded by [his admirers](#the-fangirls)
### In the new save
- He might encounter the player early on and antagonize him.
- He might have some seemingly shallow ambition of becoming a gallant knight, though this appears at first to mostly be a way to gain admiration.
- He would later be revealed to have a true heart of gold.
- When chaos befalls the world he becomes a hero in his own right.
- He might travel with the player, without his admirers.
### In the old save
- A bitter, fat slob who has let himself go.
- Sneers at couples, or at anyone who is happy.
- Believes that love ends in misery.
- No longer has those muscles he used to be so proud of.

## The Fangirls
- A group of initially shallow and identical character designs, meant to just be swooning around the casanova.
- Three identical girls.
- Possible names: Britney, Whitney and Sydney.
### In the new save
- They are first seen just swooning and "Kyaa~"ing around the [The Casanova](#the-casanova)
- Seem like incredibly shallow character archetypes at first gloss.
- After the Casanova joins the party, they are revealed to actually be characters of some depth.
- Either they are encountered and learned about later, or The Casanova speaks wistfully about them.
- They are incredibly proud of the casanova for chasing his dream of being a hero.
### In the old save
- Empty shells of their old self.
- Might be as shallow as they appeared initially to be. Defined only by their swooning.
- They no longer swoon over the casanova. Instead having attached themselves to either a memory, or to a bad person.
- The corrupted version of the casanova might have chased them out after becoming embittered.
- They might be unable to speak at all other than a fangirl swoon and squeal, affected by the corruption of the world more than most.

## The Folk Singer
- The narration and framing device of the game, later revealed to be a character in the world.
- Poetic and guiding, writes and sings songs about the events in the world.
- Might be close with [The Scribe](#the-scribe-archivist), since old songs and writings are also kept in his archives.
### In the new save
- He might be encountered during the travels of the player, on a calm camp scene.
- He might let the player listen on songs that reveal some old stories of the world.
- Has interest in the player and is optimistic about his quests, "since it would make for a good song".
### In the old save
- He is incredibly cheery and optimistic unlike all the other corrupted versions.
- Manic, a bit *too* upbeat. Wide eyed smile that doesn't break ever.
- Sings about the future. About things he's extremely certain will happen despite having no proof.
- Chief among these songs is the idea of a reunion with the hero of the old save, who left.
- Seems certain he knows why the hero of the old save left, and that he'll come back and "fix everything".
- Might try to warp the player to fit the role of the old player.
- Might be one of the final antagonists.
- Might be left behind in the world of the old save, influencing the story just through his songs that can be heard through the portals, until the player eventually makes it to him.
- Might have a strong relationship with the corrupted form of [The Gardener](#the-gardener)

## Saint Elmeda, the Saint of Lost Causes
- A radiant, maternal deity.
- A saint, which means she's somewhat of a supernatural entity.
- Despite being a saint, does not form part of any central pantheon or religion. (that's just how saints work in this world)
- Her domain is "lost causes", meaning she mostly appears to people who have lost something.
- The stories surrounding her are of times where she taught people who lost something to move on, she's a saint who shows people what they still have in the wake of disaster.
- She does not solve or give back what was lost.
- Represents the belief that "love is all you need".
### In the new save
- She appears before the player early on, granting them her support in their quest.
- It might be learned later that she is the Saint of Lost Causes, which would make it seem like the player's quest might be doomed?
- She is learned from by the stories heard of her around, and in a little shrine kept by [The Gravekeeper](#the-gravekeeper)
- [The Dragon](#the-dragon-king-vilú) might be particularly wary about her during his conquest, as if this small deity has a power only he realizes could be a threat.
### In the old save
- She is dead.
- She died shortly after the old player left.
- She might have tried and failed to convince the world to move on despite the absence of the hero.
- She might have earned the spite from the world for trying to suggest they let go of the idea of the hero.
- It is that spite that would have caused her death (maybe saints work that way?)
- Her death is a major factor in the corruption of the world of the old save.
- [The Gravekeeper](#the-gravekeeper) and [The Singer](#the-folk-singer) from the corrupted world may be trying to revive her and bring the old hero back.

## The Serpent General of Water Cai
- One of Vilú's 4 Serpent Generals.
- A sadistic strategist.
- Known for his ruthlessness.
- Clever traps, often involving poison, enjoys toying with his foes.
- In Vilú's army, he's the conqueror.
- He might be the only one of the Serpent General's that does not rule over any territories, since he's strength is in combat and strategy, and not at all in Governance.
- He might feel that sort of thing is beneath him.
### In the new save
- He starts as the Water General in Vilú's army. Like all others, he's fiercely loyal.
- He might be fought early on, as he seeks out the party, though even defeated, through trickery he runs away.
- When Vilú is killed he will turn to mournful vengeance.
- The joy of his ruthlesness might become replaced by pure vengeance, both for the [Chimera](#the-dragon-king-vilú) that replaced him, and for [The Player](#the-hero) for having kept him distracted and unable to protect his master.
### In the old save
- He is absorbed by [Chimera-Vilú](#the-dragon-king-vilú).
- The venomous blue serpent head of the chimera, he is constantly biting at the [brown serpent head](#the-serpent-geneeral-of-earth-tren), causing painful looking poisoned gashes in the body of the beast he's part of.
- Only seems vaguely aware of anything.

## The Serpent General of Earth Tren
- One of Vilú's 4 Serpent Generals.
- A stalwart defender.
- The most beloved of the 4 generals, known for keeping his people safe.
- Vilú entrusts him with defense of his territories. People conquered tend to be able to continue their lives with little effort if they're part of Tren's territories.
### In the new save
- He is fought early on, the party has to seek him out.
- The death of [Vilú](#the-dragon-king-vilú) might happen shortly before or during the fight against him.
- He might believe the corruptions that have appeared through portals were the party's doing.
- He realizes l ater they're not when he sees the party fight them as well.
- With Vilú dead, he joins the party, since the corruptions are the current threat to the people he swore to protect.
- Begins distrustful, warms up to the party.
- Mourns the death of Vilú, since he was a staunch believer that the dragon knew what to do.
- Might be in deep conflict with Cai, since Cai is not fully convinced the party didn't have anything to do with the death of Vilú.
### In the old save
- He is absorbed by [Chimera-Vilú](#the-dragon-king-vilú).
- The fortified brown serpent head of the chimera. He tries to shield the body of the beast from the vicious strikes of the [blue serpent head](#the-serpent-general-of-water-cai), as a result this is the area of the beast with the most bleeding gashes.
- Only seems vaguely aware of anything.

## The other Serpent Generals
- The serpent general of Wind, Kuyen, is associated with the moon.
- The serpent general of Flame, Antu, is associated with the sun.
- Both of them dissappeared shortly before the portals opened.
- Their dissappearances are a complete mistery.
- There is no sign of their old-save versions either.
- They exist mostly as set up to be subverted: The player is set up for the story line of killing the 4 elemental generals like a classic RPG, but the death of Vilú and the dissappearance of these two makes that quest impossible.

## The Ghost Geek
- A charismatic paranormal investigator
- Always has a bunch of gadgets and ghost finding paraphernalia on him
- His interest in ghosts is always personal
- Somewhat annoying and bombastic, a big personality that tends to annoy people
- People might describe his ghost interest as "obsessive.
### In the new save
- A schlubby guy that nobody takes very seriously.
- His methods for ghost finding are not very sound by every magical standard. Despite this, he seems to have a decent ammount of success with it.
- Despite ghosts being generally understood to not be alive, he seems to have a lot of empathy for them.
- People find him annoying, but when ghost trouble arises, they know they can call him.
- Nerdy, struggles with situational awareness. The sort of guy that will be geeking out about some ghost thing without realizing there's more important things at stake.
### In the old save
- A ruthless ghost exterminator
- Seeks out ghosts, captures him in his devices, then enjoys torturing them
- Will justify it as ghost not being really alive (he might be correct about that fact)
- Just as excited about ghosts as his other version
- His sadism is lower stakes than Cai's, but he takes delight in having power over something helpless

## The Scholar
- A glasses wearing young magical student
- Though he's not as good at casting spells or as powerful as [the Wizard Sage](#the-wizard-sage), his knowledge of the mechanisms and underlying theory of magic is very advanced
- Where [the Mentor](#the-mentor) represents the Engine and [the Sage](#the-wizard-sage) represents the Steering Wheel, the Scholar represents the map
### In the new save
- Might be an early party member
- Optimistic, slightly awkward type
- Might be accompanying the player out of a wish to study the crystals that they need to collect
- A treasure trove of random knowledge. Seems to always know something or other about every thing he's asked about.
- Might conflict with [the Ghost Geek](#the-ghost-geek) for finding his methods and lack of scientific curiosity bothersome
- Might conflict with [the Casanova](#the-casanova) for generally finding him not a very smart person overly concerned with his looks
### In the old save
- Is not a person at all, but a gigantic sprawling computer
- Turned himself into a machine bit by bit in his desire to acquire and produce more and more information
- Called the Miracle Machine by the corrupted [Scribe](#the-scribe-archivist), who obsesses over it and the endless writing and data it produces
- Might be a mid-boss along with the corrupted Scribe
- People might believe the machine has a way to stop the intrusions from the other world or even to fix the old save, but it becomes clear it cannot do any such thing
- Despite being a mindless machine, his other version can immediately tell it is himself somehow
- Might be destroyed as the aftermath of the confrontation between the party and the corrupted Scribe

## The Gardener
- An older man who cares deeply about The Saints
- Gentle demeanor, a soft smile
### In the new save
- He is found at the small shrines where the player encounters [Saint Elmeda](#saint-elmeda-the-saint-of-lost-causes)
- Might give the player some information about the saints and their role in the world
- Always working, mowing grass, cleaning statues, removing old spent candles from the shrines
- A very gentle and calming presence.
### In the old save
- Known as The Keeper of the Dead Saint
- Completely unseen until the very end of the game
- He has stayed in the old world, one of few characters that didn't go through the opened portals
- Despite the old world being ruined and the saint being dead, has faith that the player of the other save will return, and that the Saint will be restored
- The shrine to lady Elmeda, small and understated in the other world, has become a gigantic cathedral in th old save by his care
- Toxic and mutually enabling relationship with [the corrupted Singer](#the-folk-singer), his beliefs are partly spurred on by his delusional songs
- Upon meeting the new player in the cathedral, might try to twist them and turn them against their will into the old player who left.
- This attempt might be the final boss of the game
- Represents the opposite reaction to grief as [the corrupted Sage](#the-wizard-sage), where the sage has given up on the world and tried to enact reckless change, the Keeper of the Dead Saint tries to preserve the old world unchanged, to freeze it until the player comes back.
