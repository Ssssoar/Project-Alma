# Character List
This will be a list of characters and their concepts, as well as possible relationships, story significance and arcs.

Because of the [two-world structure](01-concept.md) of the world, most characters will have two versions; one from the old save (the corrupted version) and one from the new save.

Both versions of the characters are understood to stem from the same branching points, with the events of each world/save file being what led them on the path to become each version of themselves. For the old save, the abandonment of the player is the biggest catalyst.

Most characters are listed by their role/archetype in the story rather than having a name.

## The Mentor
- Forward moving, a man of action.
- Extremely goal oriented.
- Always keeping his objectives in mind, always striving to fulfill them.
### In the new save
- He is a stoic guide.
- Pushes the player towards the original quest, to defeat the dragon that tries to conquer the land.
- Once the dragon dies and the corruptions appear he might develop a rigid attitude, trying to make sure you kill them as much as possible.
- The encounter with his old-save version might change this attitude to something more reasonable.
### In the old save
- He is a wild eyed, desperate seeker.
- Wants at all costs to find the player of the old save.
- He might become quite wicked in the search of this objective, since it's an end that justifies all means in his head.
- Senses something of the player of the old save in the one of the new one.

## The Dragon King Vilú
- A sly conqueror.
- Defined by his ambition.
- A representation of resentment and bitterness, and how they can act as a driving force for good if controlled.
- Loved by his subordinates. Despite being seen as evil, is actually a very good ruler and protector of his lands.
- Just that, you know, they're HIS lands.
- Has a very close relationship to his immediate subordinates, the four Serpent Generals
### In the new save
- He is the inciting event for the player's adventure.
- He is trying to conquer all the land.
- The quest to defeat him takes much of the early game.
- He is unceremoniously killed by his counterpart when the intrussions begin to happen.
- His loss leaves most of his lands in disarray.
- Might only be a fakeout, he would appear as an ally towards the end of the game.
### In the old save
- Known as Chimera-Vilú
- He is a hulking, monstrous, unthinking abomination.
- He has fused with his minions and two of his serpent generals.
- Like a monster, it has no objective.
- Has a dragon head and 2 serpent heads, those of his absorbed generals.
- The three heads constantly fight and hurt each other, and as a result the Chimera is in constant bleeding pain.
- Killing it's counterpart is the first thing it does as soon as it enters the new world.
- Might be the final boss of Act 2.
- There might be a misunderstanding about whether he is leading the corruptions from the other world, that would be resolved when upon his death, the chaos doesn't stop.

## The Wizard Sage
- A wise man and a recluse.
- Lives somewhere hard to reach and away from people.
- Maybe in a tall wizard tower in a cliff by a raging sea.
- Known as an incredibly powerful mage, though his existance might not be certain to people.
- His trust in the player is his most important aspect for the story.
### In the new save
- As part of the original quest to defeat the dragon, he was supposed to entrust the player some wisdom or secret that would aid him. Maybe the Dragon's weakness.
- With the Dragon defated, his secret doesn't have much use.
- It might be that the player seeks him hoping he can help with the intrussions, only to be disappointed when he can't or won't.
- His encounter with his alternate version might give him some insight as to what is going on with the intrusions from the other world.
### In the old save
- Affected by the abandonment of the player of the old save
- Had at first decided to have faith that the old player would return, but after enough time had passed and the dragon became a hulking abomination, decided to take things in his own hands.
- He develops and uses the most powerful magic in the world to open a portal to "a different past"
- He is the reason the abominations are flowing into the new save: The portals he opened are what's making everything flow towards it.
- He did this out of a belief that the player could be found in the new save, and fix things.
- Crumbles when he realizes the player of the new save is not the one that had abandoned his.
- Plagued by his guilt, since he believes he has doomed both worlds with his actions.

## The Scribe Archivist
- A man concerned with documents and writings.
- Might own or keep a library, has a strong belief in the power and importance of keeping knowledge.
- A very knowledgeable person, though not really an expert in many topics.
### In the new save
- A calm presence.
- Might have a sidequest involving bringing him books and archives from around the world.
- Might be a source of information for various legends, particularly regarding the saints, and rumors about the wizard.
- Forced to flee his library once the intrusions from the other world begin appearing.
### In the old save
- A maniacal hoarder of any and every scrap of writing.
- Has a mistaken belief that somwhere, somehow, there's a piece of writing that he can discover that can restore his old broken world.
- Burdened by his papers and books, yet refuses to let go of even a little bit.
- Forms a toxic relationship to the corrupted form of [The Scholar](#the-scholar).
- Grows increasingly frustrated as none of the writing that he hoards and reads brings him any closer to solving the problem in his world.
- Eventually lashes out, burning all the writing that he so jealously kept.
- After the fires are set to the library, he panics and regrets it.
- He might try to save everything from the fire.
- He forever might mourn everything he lost in the fire.
- He might have gone through this cycle of hoarding -> burning -> regret many times in the past.

## The Casanova
- A Boisterous, over-the-top ladies' man.
- Big muscles, chiseled jaw, Johnny Bravo type swagger.
- Loves to flex, loves to work out, loves to show off.
- Playfully antagonistic and competitive.
- Surrounded by [his admirers](#the-fangirls)
### In the new save
- He might encounter the player early on and antagonize him.
- He might have some seemingly shallow ambition of becoming a gallant knight, though this appears at first to mostly be a way to gain admiration.
- He would later be revealed to have a true heart of gold.
- When chaos befalls the world he becomes a hero in his own right.
- He might travel with the player, without his admirers.
### In the old save
- A bitter, fat slob who has let himself go.
- Sneers at couples, or at anyone who is happy.
- Believes that love ends in misery.
- No longer has those muscles he used to be so proud of.

## The Fangirls
- A group of initially shallow and identical character designs, meant to just be swooning around the casanova.
- Three identical girls.
- Possible names: Britney, Whitney and Sydney.
### In the new save
- They are first seen just swooning and "Kyaa~"ing around the [The Casanova](#the-casanova)
- Seem like incredibly shallow character archetypes at first gloss.
- After the Casanova joins the party, they are revealed to actually be characters of some depth.
- Either they are encountered and learned about later, or The Casanova speaks wistfully about them.
- They are incredibly proud of the casanova for chasing his dream of being a hero.
### In the old save
- Empty shells of their old self.
- Might be as shallow as they appeared initially to be. Defined only by their swooning.
- They no longer swoon over the casanova. Instead having attached themselves to either a memory, or to a bad person.
- The corrupted version of the casanova might have chased them out after becoming embittered.
- They might be unable to speak at all other than a fangirl swoon and squeal, affected by the corruption of the world more than most.

## The Folk Singer
- The narration and framing device of the game, later revealed to be a character in the world.
- Poetic and guiding, writes and sings songs about the events in the world.
- Might be close with [The Scribe](#the-scribe-archivist), since old songs and writings are also kept in his archives.
### In the new save
- He might be encountered during the travels of the player, on a calm camp scene.
- He might let the player listen on songs that reveal some old stories of the world.
- Has interest in the player and is optimistic about his quests, "since it would make for a good song".
### In the old save
- Unlike most of the corrupted versions
